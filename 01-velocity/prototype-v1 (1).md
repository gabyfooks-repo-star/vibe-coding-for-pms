## Show & Swap read

_What a partner understood from your build with no verbal setup, their reaction is your first piece of product evidence._

- **What they understood immediately:** It is an independent wedding planner website to reach audience digitally and get more bookings
- **What confused them:** The heading a sample of Noor's work.. can be made more catchy and direct
- **The assumption they thought you were testing:** If the booking rates increase by creating this website more than just handing over physical cards in person
- **The gap between what you intended and what they read:** Pretty good read! The main point was to help someone get more leads without existing review, so there was a bit of a gap in communicating this.
